# My Favorite Anime

## Horimiya

**Horimiya** is my favorite anime because it shows friendship, love, and the importance of accepting people for who they really are.

### About the Anime
Horimiya is a romantic comedy anime about two students, Kyouko Hori and Izumi Miyamura. They discover each other's different sides outside school and eventually become close.

### Why I Like It
- The story is simple and enjoyable.
- The characters have interesting personalities.
- It shows the importance of friendship and relationships.
- It has funny and heartwarming moments.

### My Favorite Characters
**Kyouko Hori** - She is cheerful, responsible, and caring.

**Izumi Miyamura** - He is quiet at first but becomes more open and comfortable around others.

### My Favorite Part
My favorite part is how Hori and Miyamura slowly become closer and learn to accept each other.

### My Rating
**10/10** - I would definitely recommend this anime to people who enjoy romance and comedy!
